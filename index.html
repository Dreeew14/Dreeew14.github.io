About me

A steady pair of hands behind every transaction.

For more than 15 years I've worked with clients in BPO, customer service, sales and real estate. In that time I've learned one thing: people relax when they know someone has their back.

Most recently I've been coordinating property transactions from start to finish. I chase documents and deadlines, work alongside agents, builders, developers, solicitors and property managers, and keep clients updated at every step, so nobody is left wondering what happens next.

I love keeping things organized and following through. I can handle several files at once, solve problems as they come up, and work on my own in a remote setup. Now I want to bring that to your team as your Transaction Coordinator and all-round property support.
