01 About

A steady pair of hands behind every file.

Clients relax when they know someone is on top of the details. For more than 10 years, making sure of that has been my job.

Most recently I was Transaction & Settlement Coordinator for an Australian property team. I managed 25–40 live files at a time for 200+ clients, including multi-property investors, and kept 6–10 parties per deal moving: agents, builders, developers, solicitors and property managers.

Before that, I booked buyer and listing appointments for a US Berkshire Hathaway HomeServices team, and spent nearly six years at Wells Fargo investigating disputes to banking compliance standards. So I know both sides of the deal: bringing clients in, and getting them to the finish line, accurately.

I'm organized, I follow through, and I keep everyone in the loop without being asked. You'll always know where every file stands.

07 FAQ

Questions, answered.

Have you worked with US and Australian teams?

Yes. I supported listing and buyer agents for a US real estate team, and managed client relations and property transactions for an Australian property team. I'm used to working across both markets and their different processes.

What hours do you work?

I'm based in Manila and available for Australian and US business hours. We'll agree on your schedule and response times during our first call.

Do you work part-time or full-time?

Both. Some clients need a full-time Transaction Coordinator, others need a few hours a day for client updates and admin. Tell me what you need and I'll suggest a setup that fits.

What do you need from me to get started?

Access to the tools you already use (CRM, email, e-sign, shared drive), your checklists or templates if you have them, and a short walkthrough of how you like things done. I'll take it from there.

How do you keep me updated?

However suits you best: a daily summary in Slack or email, a shared tracker, or a quick weekly call. You'll always know what's done, what's pending and what needs your attention.

Ready to hand off your next file?

A 30-minute call to see if we're a good fit. No pressure, no obligation.
